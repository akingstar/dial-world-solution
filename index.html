FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions before you get started?

Here are answers to common setup and service questions.

Can you install and configure a complete VICIdial system?

Yes. Available scope can include server preparation, installation, extensions, campaigns, dialer settings and agreed test checks.

Can you configure my SIP trunk or VoIP carrier?

We can assist with compatible SIP provider configuration and troubleshooting. Carrier approval, account charges and call rates are separate.

Do you provide VPS hosting?

Managed hosting options can be discussed based on agent capacity, storage, traffic and server requirements. Third-party infrastructure charges may apply.

Can you configure AMD, IVR and predictive dialing?

Supported VICIdial and Asterisk features can be configured according to your version, server resources, carrier capabilities and operational requirements.

Do you provide AI voice bots and CRM integration?

These are custom-scope services. Feasibility, third-party API requirements and ongoing usage charges are confirmed before work begins.

Are the listed prices final?

Listed prices are introductory service fees. Custom work, VPS, SIP trunks, phone numbers, call minutes, taxes and third-party services may cost extra.

How do I get started?

Submit the enquiry form with your requirements. We will review the scope and discuss suitable next steps before payment.
